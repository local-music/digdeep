Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how DIG DEEP – Mining Adventure handles information when you use the app.

Gameplay data

DIG DEEP saves gameplay progress, including currency balances, upgrades and unlocks, locally on your device. No account is required to play.

Advertising

DIG DEEP integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, subject to applicable law and the ad settings and consent choices in the app. This data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalisation. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob privacy guidance.

In-app purchases

Optional coin, gem and bundle purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. DIG DEEP receives transaction information needed to validate purchases and deliver in-game items. We do not receive your full payment card number. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request.

Children and age-appropriate advertising

DIG DEEP is a cartoon game. Advertising behaviour and privacy disclosures must be configured to match the audience and age-rating settings selected for the published app. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Sharing and retention

Locally saved gameplay data stays on your device unless removed by you or your device's operating system. Advertising and purchase providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Support emails are retained only as reasonably needed to address requests and related obligations.

Your choices

Rewarded ads and purchases are optional. You can manage device privacy permissions and Apple purchase settings in iOS Settings. You may delete local game data by removing the app, subject to iOS backup behaviour. Contact us for questions about support emails or privacy.

Updates

We may update this policy when DIG DEEP's features or third-party services change. The date at the top indicates the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@kook.love.
