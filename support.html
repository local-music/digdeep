Support

Need help with DIG DEEP? We're here to help.

Contact

Email us at support@kook.love. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Start a run, drag to steer your drill, collect ores and avoid obstacles. Use coins and gems to upgrade your equipment.

My rewarded ad isn't available

Rewarded ads are optional and may not always be available due to internet connectivity, ad availability or other restrictions. Try again later; you can keep playing without watching an ad.

I purchased coins or gems but didn't receive them

Check that the transaction completed in Apple's purchase sheet. If your balance didn't update, restart the app and contact support with the product name, approximate time and your Apple order reference if available. Do not send your Apple ID password.

Can I play offline?

Core gameplay is designed to work without an account. Advertisements and in-app purchases require network access.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com.
