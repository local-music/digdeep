About DIG DEEP

DIG DEEP is a single-player cartoon mining game for iPhone. Collect coins and gems, unlock drilling machines, explore new worlds and enjoy daily rewards.

The game offers optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded advertisements. You can play without creating an account.
